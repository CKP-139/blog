Mark Soskin - Excavation, from Keys of the city.

Mark Soskin is not really a game composer. He's an American jazz pianist who has performed as a sideman to Sonny Rollins, Herbie Mann, and others. However, in April 1990, he was involved in the recording of Keys of the city, an arrangement album for the KOEI business management sim Leading Company. (This album was released 25 October 1990, but all of the release dates I can find on databases say the game came out in 1992... I suspect that the game was initially released in 1990, but I haven't been able to find anything after a cursory search...)

Soskin's contributions to this project were arrangements for all tracks, and composing the songs Excavation (embedded above) and [The door to September](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JbucDQZEjME) — the former being an uptempo bebop tune with a rollicking sax solo, and the latter being a Metheny-esque fusion track.

Interestingly, as pointed out by my friend jaxcheese, the [in-game versions of these songs](https://youtu.be/--Gxf09LVxs?t=1131) (timestamped to Excavation at 18:51) also have direct transcriptions of the solos performed on the album version. For this reason, it seems likely that the live album was recorded first, and the in-game versions created after the live ones. 

KOEI's [Soundware Division](https://vgmdb.net/artist/6451) released a handful of other fully produced and arranged albums around this time — notably,  the Nobunaga's Ambition, Uncharted Waters, and Romance of the Three Kingdoms series would get entries with full treatments. However, Leading Company is a standout in its use of American session players recording in New York City, many of whom would call this album their sole foray into game music.

The rest of this album and soundtrack is extremely jazzy and fun to listen to, so please check it out!
